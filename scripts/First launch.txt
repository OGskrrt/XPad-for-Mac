XPad — first launch
======================

1. Drag XPad into Applications and open it.
2. macOS says it can't verify the app (XPad is free and not paid-signed by Apple).
   Click "Done", then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down
   and click "Open Anyway". You only do this once.
3. A controller icon appears in the menu bar. Plug in your controller.
4. To use it as keyboard & mouse, allow XPad in
   System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

Help: https://ogskrrt.github.io/XPad-for-Mac/
